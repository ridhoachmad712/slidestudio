# Penyamaan kebutuhan dosen

Read for a new lecture or a changed audience, teaching approach, depth, or unclear brief. Do not repeat elicitation for a small edit with established requirements.

## Resolve the brief

Accept ordinary language, RPS, readings, source decks, and design examples. Extract information already supplied before asking questions. Use `course/brief.md` in the kit, or equivalent notes outside it. Do not require every field to be filled.

Separate content, teaching approach, and visual presentation. Record audience/prerequisites, outcomes, scope/exclusions, duration, count, sources, expansion mode, slide function, depth, examples/assessment, formats, and design constraints. Keep unknowns separate from assumptions. Ask concise, bundled questions only about gaps that materially change the result. Read sources while optional answers are pending. When asked to finish directly, state reasonable assumptions and proceed unless a mandatory conflict requires an answer. Never attribute invented outcomes to an unread RPS.

## Priority and conflicts

Current meeting instructions take priority over stored preferences and design defaults. RPS outcomes, required references, institutional requirements, and numerical limits are requirements within their stated scope. An institutional template controls design when requested. A source deck controls content when supplied for that purpose, not automatically design.

Explicit corrections can supersede earlier preferences. If mandatory requirements conflict without a clear replacement, state the concrete conflict and ask a targeted question. Continue unaffected work. Do not silently drop an outcome or essential explanation to fit an incompatible limit. Source evidence controls factual accuracy regardless of visual or content preferences.

## Source expansion modes

- **Setia pada sumber:** reorganize and clarify supplied content without new topics, external factual claims, or original cases. Retain caveats and flag gaps. A specific request to verify or correct a fact permits that bounded verification.
- **Pengembangan terbatas:** add supporting explanation, original illustrative examples, and exercises within the supplied scope. Label invented cases/data and distinguish additions from source content in notes. Avoid unsupported factual claims and syllabus expansion.
- **Pengembangan dengan riset:** add verified sources to support the requested outcomes, preserve scope, and cite additions. Permission to research does not authorize publishing or uploading private materials.

If substantial source material is supplied without a mode, use limited development within its scope and disclose the assumption. A request to preserve content exactly means source-faithful mode. An explicit research request authorizes that mode within its scope. Ask when ambiguity affects required content. Do not present an original example as a textbook example.

## Slide function and depth

- **Pendamping ceramah:** show concepts and evidence students need, with extended explanation and prompts in notes.
- **Belajar mandiri:** provide terms, reasoning, assumptions, and examples that are understandable without oral narration. Essential explanation cannot live only in notes.
- **Gabungan:** keep slides understandable after class and add lecturer guidance in notes.

Translate “lengkap” into topic-appropriate coverage. Mechanisms need how/why, calculations need notation and reasoning steps, and decisions need criteria, consequences, and limits. Do not mechanically add all categories to every slide. Agree what belongs in slides versus notes. Add slides or a handout only when compatible with the request. No fixed word count should force unreadable text.

## Visual understanding and optional samples

Inspect references for fonts, density, hierarchy, composition, imagery, and language. Separate inspiration from exact template matching. Translate vague requests into concrete choices. Creative design should retain readability and does not require images on every slide.

When depth or style is unsettled, offer three representative slides: concept explanation, case/application, and evidence/calculation. A cover alone is insufficient. This stage is optional. Respect requests to complete directly and existing approvals. Apply feedback to the relevant whole-deck patterns, not just the sample slides.

## Lecturer profile

Use a supplied `course/lecturer-profile.md`. Record preference, scope, status, evidence/date, and superseding correction. **Langsung** means stated by the lecturer. **Sementara** means inferred, usable only as a reversible default when meeting instructions are absent. **Dikonfirmasi** means explicitly accepted by the lecturer for the recorded broader scope.

Do not make one-deck fonts permanent defaults. Store reusable preferences only when requested or accepted for future use. Avoid sensitive personal inferences. Allow corrections and prioritize meeting instructions. Persistent use requires an actual profile file accessible later; the skill alone does not provide cross-chat or cross-app memory.

## Coverage and review

Use `course/coverage-map.md` when present or equivalent planning notes. Link each outcome to explanation, application, assessment, and gaps. Keep this map outside student slides unless requested. Flag outcomes that cannot realistically be achieved within the duration.

Review against the resolved brief: scope, audience, depth, slide function, approach, timing, design, sources, formats, and editability. State assumptions and untested aspects honestly. Translate feedback such as longer, more practical, or less formal into concrete revisions. Structural validation cannot certify instructional alignment.
